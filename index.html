How it works: JobRadar pulls live remote roles from Remotive, We Work Remotely, Jobicy and RemoteOK, and jobs near your city from Jooble once its key is in config.json. Every card is a real vacancy and Apply opens the original posting. Under Companies you can add employers you like; JobRadar then reads their career pages directly. LinkedIn and Indeed do not allow apps to search for you, so the rows below open their search pages with your terms already filled in. Fill in your profile and click Find Jobs. Jobs you have not seen before get a green edge and a NEW badge.
Remote listings courtesy of Remotive, Remote OK, Jobicy and We Work Remotely. Local listings via the Jooble API when configured. Apply always opens the original posting.
